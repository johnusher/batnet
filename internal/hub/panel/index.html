              This hub

              Away note

              Only to someone who writes to this hub alone, never on a channel. Once per radio a week, at most 10 a day. Not to a bare "ok", "danke" or 👍, not to a message from a machine, and not to a radio that asked not to be written to. Anything you send them first cancels it.

              Log

              Records every packet the radio hears, even those too weak to read. It keeps going after a restart or a power cut until you stop it: take the hub along on a trip.
