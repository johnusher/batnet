## 2026-10-24
* **Creation**: Research note [what Meshtastic does for privacy, keys and trust](/research/meshtastic-security.md), from a friend's remark that batnet "needs a privacy layer, key exchange mechanisms, chain of trust": channels are one shared AES-CTR key anyone holding it can read and forge, direct messages are X25519 and AES-256-CCM since 2.5 (the roof hub's *error 39* is a missing public key), broadcasts are signed since 2.8, keys are trusted on first sight with no chain of trust, headers are never hidden, and the 2025 advisories (cloned and weak keys, a downgrade to the channel key). batnet's private channel rides the channel layer, which is B51 exactly; the chain of trust is batnet's to build, added to [ideas](/ideas.md).
